# PropHunt for Garry's Mod 📦🔍

## 🐛 Known Issues

* **Awkward Prop Positioning:** Props can occasionally have an awkward position, angle, or alignment when picked up by a player.
* **Taunt Menu Audio:** The taunt menu is currently bugged and is not playing all of the available sounds.
* **HUD/UI Overlap:** Certain HUD elements or UI menus are overlapping each other during gameplay.

---

## 🔮 Future Implementation

Here is a look at what is planned for future updates to our game:

- [ ] **Decoy System:** Give Props the ability to spawn a fake, static copy of themselves to trick Hunters.
- [ ] **Simpler Score HUD:** A cleaner, simplified heads-up display for tracking scores during the match.
- [ ] **Prop Powerups:** Give Props the ability to use consumable powerups, such as flashbangs or stuns, to escape Hunters.
- [ ] **Hunter Playermodel Menu:** A dedicated selection menu allowing players on the Hunter team to choose their preferred playermodel.
